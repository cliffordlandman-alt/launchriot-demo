# LaunchRiot live demo (universal)

A public, static demo of a LaunchRiot workspace for South African small businesses: [launchriot.online](https://launchriot.online).

- Pick an industry (trades, property and holiday homes, financial advisers, clinics and salons, retail and e-commerce, professional services, logistics) to swap the sample business, jobs, wording and bot conversations.
- Five AI staff bots: Support, Sales, Marketing, Dispatch/Operations and Finance. In the demo they only draft; every action waits for approval.
- Every business, person, figure and message is fictional sample data. Nothing is sent, posted or charged. Data is stored only in your browser and resets every 24 hours.

Live: https://cliffordlandman-alt.github.io/launchriot-demo/
